PBGC §436 — Intake & Review

v0.3.0 · Works from file:// without internet, installation, or external libraries.

Review-only prototype. This tool documents case evidence, checks AFTAP coverage, and prepares draft tables. It does not calculate statutory presumptions, make final benefit determinations, or alter the official PBGC DOCX template. No participant PII should be entered.

1. Case identity and dates

2. Part 1 — Screening

Choose Yes, No, or N/A only after reviewing evidence. Unknown means investigation remains open. Record an IPS/document reference and a reason for each conclusion.

3. Part 2 — AFTAP certification inventory

Enter one certification per line, including recertifications and range certifications. A source document is required to verify each value. For missing certifications, enter an empty AFTAP and explain in notes.

AFTAP: number (65.48) or range (60-80, <60, >=80). Date: YYYY-MM-DD. CSV quotation supported. Put range/recertification/missing/assumed in cert_type.

4. Part 3 — Effective AFTAP periods

Enter independently substantiated actual and presumed periods. The application will detect overlaps, gaps, and missing coverage; it will not infer that a missing certification means no restriction.

5. Part 4 — Restriction analysis and case evidence

Do not automatically equate low AFTAP with an affected benefit. Capture the specific event, benefit form, accrual period, payment, and administrator action.
